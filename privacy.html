Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Cape Island Recovery is a program of Inspiration Is Everywhere Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Falmouth, MA. Privacy and anonymity matter in recovery. This page explains what we collect and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our database provider; we can't see your password).
  • Requests and volunteer sign-ups: what you ask for, your town, how you'd like to be contacted, a phone number if you give one, and any note you add.
  • Pledges: your email, optional name, what you'd like to give and any message.

Please don't send medical or treatment details through the site. We'll talk privately.

How we use it

Only to respond to you, deliver food, connect you to resources, coordinate volunteers and follow up on pledges. We never sell, rent or publish your information, and we never post about anyone we serve without their written permission.

Who can see it

You can see your own requests when signed in. A small number of authorized team members can see requests so they can help. Access is enforced by database security rules. With your permission, we may share what's needed with a partner (for example, a food pantry) to get you help.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use advertising trackers.

Deleting your data

Signed in? Use "Delete all my data from this site" on your account page. Or email and we'll delete your information and your login.

Crisis

This site is not for emergencies. Call or text 988, or call 911.
