Our mission

Cape Island Recovery moves food and opens doors. Based in Falmouth, MA, we deliver meals and groceries to people in recovery and neighbors in need, and we connect people in recovery to the resources they need to rebuild: benefits, treatment, housing and meetings.

We meet people where they are, without judgment. Whether you're on day one or year ten, or you're a neighbor who's just having a hard month, you're welcome here.

What we do

Food transport

We move food to people who need it: meals and groceries for people in recovery and neighbors in need, plus pick-ups from food donors and runs to and from local food pantries.

Resource connection

We help people in recovery find and reach the right support: benefits like SNAP, MassHealth and fuel help, treatment options, housing, and recovery meetings. Check what you may qualify for with the free Root to Rise benefits navigator.

How to get help

  1. Reach out. Call , email us, or create a free account and send a request.
  2. Tell us what you need. Food, help finding resources, or both. You don't have to explain your story, and you don't have to be in a program.
  3. We follow up privately, the way you asked us to (call, text or email).

Hours, delivery area and how often we deliver are coming soon. For now, call or send a request and we'll tell you what we can do.

Need help right now? Call or text 988 any time. In an emergency, call 911.
